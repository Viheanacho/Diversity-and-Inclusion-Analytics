**Diversity-and-Inclusion-Analytics**

**1. Project Headline**
Diversity & Inclusion Analytics:An Interactive Power BI Dashboard for Workforce Representation, Promotion Equity, and Retention Analysis

**2. Description / Purpose**:
This project was carried out to provide a data-driven evaluation of **organizational equity and representation**. By leveraging an open-source dataset, a comprehensive analytical framework was constructed to monitor demographic trends and identify potential biases within the corporate lifecycle.
The primary objective of this analysis was the transformation of workforce data into actionable insights regarding **Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI)**. A multi-dimensional approach was adopted to ensure that representation was assessed not only by headcount but also through the lens of **career progression, promotion, performance, and employee retention**.
The dashboard allows stakeholders to explore DEI metrics across different **departments, job levels, age groups, and regions**, providing a clearer view of how employees progress through different stages of the corporate lifecycle.

**3. Tech Stack**
The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:
* **Power BI Desktop**: Used to build the interactive DEI dashboard, create visualizations, and present key workforce metrics.
* **Power Query**: Used for data cleaning, transformation, and standardization of workforce data.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Used to create calculated measures, KPIs, gender representation metrics, promotion analysis, and turnover calculations.
* **Data Modeling**: Used to structure the workforce dataset to support analysis across departments, job levels, regions, age groups, and gender.
These tools enabled the transformation of raw workforce records into an interactive analytical solution for evaluating diversity, equity, career progression, and retention.

**4. Data Source**:
The analysis was based on an **open-source Diversity and Inclusion / HR dataset** containing demographic identifiers, job levels, employee performance history, and employment information.
The dataset provided information that supported analysis across areas such as:
* Gender
* Department
* Job level
* Age group
* Region
* Employee performance ratings
* Promotion history
* Employee turnover
* Time spent within job grades

**Data Acquisition**:
An open-source HR dataset containing demographic identifiers, job levels, and performance history was sourced for the analysis.

**Data Cleaning and Transformation**:
Data was standardized to ensure consistent categorization of **gender, department, job levels, and other workforce metrics**. The dataset was prepared to support comparisons between demographic groups throughout different stages of the employee lifecycle.

**Comparative Analysis**:
Statistical benchmarks were established to compare the representation and progression of demographic groups against the overall organizational average.
This allowed the analysis to move beyond simple workforce headcount and examine whether differences existed in **hiring, promotion, career progression, performance, and turnover**.

5. Features and Highlights
The following key analytical areas were prioritized and visualized within the dashboard:

**Executive Gender Balance**
The distribution of gender across senior leadership and executive roles was scrutinized to assess the **representation of women and men at different organizational levels**. The analysis helps identify potential representation gaps as employees progress from junior positions toward senior management and executive roles, providing insight into the potential "glass ceiling" effect.

**Hiring Trends**
Recruitment data was analyzed to determine the inclusivity of the hiring pipeline and understand gender representation among new hires. The dashboard highlights the proportion of female and male hires across different job levels, allowing stakeholders to identify where representation may be improving or where further intervention may be required.

**Promotion Equity**
The frequency and distribution of promotions were compared across demographic groups to identify potential disparities in career advancement.
The dashboard specifically examines **promotions during the year**, including the representation of women and men receiving promotions across different job levels.
This provides an additional perspective on whether employees have equitable opportunities to progress within the organization.

**Career Progression**:
Employee progression was examined through **average time spent within job grades**. By comparing the average time in grade for female and male employees, the dashboard provides insight into whether there are differences in the speed of career progression across gender groups.

This complements the promotion analysis by looking not only at whether employees are promoted, but also at how long employees typically remain within a particular job level.

**Performance Rating Analysis**:
Internal performance appraisals were evaluated to examine whether performance ratings differed between demographic groups.
The dashboard compares the average performance ratings of employees who left the organization against those who remained, providing additional context for understanding the relationship between performance, retention, and employee demographics.

Turnover Rate:
Employee turnover patterns were monitored to identify whether specific demographic groups experienced disproportionately high levels of attrition.
The analysis compares turnover and performance patterns across job levels and gender, helping to identify potential retention concerns within different segments of the workforce.

Interactive Dashboard:
An interactive dashboard was designed to allow stakeholders to filter and explore DEI insights by:
* Department
* Job Level
* Age Group
* Region
This filtering capability allows users to move from an overall organizational view into more specific workforce segments and identify areas where representation, promotion, performance, or turnover may require further investigation.

Conclusion
Through this project, it was demonstrated that *diversity is a metric of both recruitment and retention*. By visualizing the relationship between *gender representation, hiring, promotion rates, career progression, performance ratings, and turnover*, the dashboard provides a broader perspective on organizational equity throughout the employee lifecycle.
The analysis establishes a clearer path toward a more equitable corporate culture by highlighting potential representation and progression gaps and identifying areas that may require further investigation or intervention.
